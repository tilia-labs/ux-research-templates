# Changelog

What's new in [tilia](https://tilialabs.io), newest first.

Entries before October 2026 were reconstructed from development history, so they are grouped by month and dated by when the work was finished, which can be a little earlier than when it reached everyone.

## October 2026

### New

- **Tags your team owns.** Every place you tag an insight now offers your own tags, including when saving from an AI summary. The default tags can be renamed or removed. (13 Oct)
- **Admin-managed tags for teams.** Renaming, merging and deleting tags is limited to team admins. Admins can also switch on "Only admins can add new tags", after which members choose from the list and suggest additions for an admin to review. (13 Oct)
- **Save AI themes and findings whenever you like.** Themes and findings from an AI summary can be saved to the Repository on any later visit, not only straight after generating. The save step remembers what you've already saved. (13 Oct)

### Improved

- Filtering the Repository by several tags now narrows the results to insights that carry all of them. (13 Oct)
- Dashboard study lists stack as cards on mobile. (8 Oct)

## August 2026

### New

- **Teams.** Invite colleagues into a shared organisation with admin and member roles, on the Team plan. Studies, the dashboard and the Repository are shared across the team. (11 Aug)
- **Reusable invite links.** Share one link with your team, alongside per-email invites. (21 Aug)
- **Your branding on studies.** Add your logo and accent colour to participant pages, and give a study a custom, readable link. (21 Aug)
- **Move a study between workspaces** from the study's menu. (21 Aug)
- Team admins can rename their organisation. (21 Aug)
- People who sign in by email link can set their name. (19 Aug)

### Improved

- Everyone in a Team or Enterprise organisation gets Pro features. (19 Aug)
- Recent studies appear as a table on the dashboard. (20 Aug)
- Billing settings are split into your current plan and your options for changing it. (14 Aug)

### Fixed

- New studies are created in the workspace you're working in, not always the default one. (21 Aug)
- The dashboard no longer flashes up before the onboarding survey for new accounts. (19 Aug)

## July 2026

### New

- **Paid plans.** Pro is available to buy from the pricing page, with plan and billing details in Settings. (13 Jul)
- **Study settings.** Cap the number of responses, pause a study, randomise order, allow one response per device, protect a study with a password, and set it up for Prolific. (14 Jul)
- **Study Assistant.** Describe what you want to learn and tilia drafts the study for you. This replaces the earlier in-builder Copilot. (14 Jul)
- **A dashboard home page.** Recent studies, studies that need attention, your usage, and themes that recur across your studies. (16 Jul)
- **Repository insight view and tag management.** Open any insight to see and edit its tags, and manage all your tags on one page. (16 Jul)
- **Sign in by email link**, with no password. (22 Jul)
- **Sign in with Microsoft.** (22 Jul)
- **Recruit participants from Prolific** without leaving tilia, with a cost estimate before you order. (28 Jul)
- **AI summaries per section.** For studies with sections or tasks, get a summary under each one as well as an overall summary. (28 Jul)
- **A welcome and consent tab** in the study builder, and a simpler markdown import with a resizable editor. (10 Jul)

### Improved

- AI summaries scale the number of themes and findings to the size of the study. (28 Jul)
- Survey section headings appear in results and in shared stakeholder reports. (28 Jul)
- After signing in you're returned to the page you were trying to reach. (28 Jul)
- Saving AI insights to the Repository is a one-at-a-time review. (16 Jul)
- The dashboard loads faster. (28 Jul)

### Fixed

- AI summaries now read responses correctly for every study type. (16 Jul)

## June 2026

### New

- **Consent, screeners and survey branching.** Ask for consent up front, screen participants out with qualifying questions, and send survey participants down different paths based on their answers. (22 Jun)
- **Preview a study** from the builder to try the participant experience without recording a response. (22 Jun)
- **Survey sections** to break a long survey into groups of questions. (29 Jun)
- **Surveys on the Free plan.** (29 Jun)
- **Demo mode.** Explore sample studies and results without creating an account. (7 Jun)

### Improved

- Accessibility: clearer keyboard focus, properly labelled fields, better contrast, and no keyboard traps. (6 Jun)
- The AI summary reads as a single column on mobile. (9 Jun)

### Fixed

- Removing one item from a list in the survey builder no longer removes them all. (7 Jun)
- A participant who is screened out can no longer get back in with the browser's Back button. (22 Jun)
- Several page-scrolling problems. (7 Jun)
